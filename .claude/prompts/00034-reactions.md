Let's resolve issue 34 with a reactions feature.

This will add a footer at the bottom of any profile page: performer profile, team profile, or coach profile. The 
footer will show any emoji reactions and allow visitors to add new ones.

Constraints:

- Please suggest emojis you think users would want on these pages. My initial pitch is to keep the tone light and 
  positive with: 
  - Like (thumb_up)
  - Love (heart)
  - Celebrate (hands_clapping_line)

- Let's use mingcute icons instead of unicode emojis, unless there is an icon library that fits better with our 
  design aesthetic

- Let's fit these in with our overall design feel in terms of look and feel, color, etc.

- Let's require users to log in in order to add reactions. Any given user can add at most 1 of each reaction. 
  Clicking again removes the reaction. 

- The footer shows the total count of all reactions but not who has given those reactions. 

Please ask any questions you have then let's make a plan.
